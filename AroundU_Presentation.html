The Market Friction

Current challenges vs. The Around-U Solution

Endless Calling

"I called three plumbers. One didn't pick up, two are busy until next week."

✓ Around-U Solution

Hyperlocal Pincode Indexing. View real-time availability in your neighborhood in seconds.

Random Pricing

"He quoted ₹500 on the phone but asked for ₹1200 after arriving."

✓ Around-U Solution

Upfront Transparency. Lock your price with the provider before the work starts.

The Trust Gap

"Allowing strangers into your home is scary. How do I know they are safe?"

✓ 100% Verified IDs

Mandatory ID and phone verification for every provider before they can join.

Our Solution

Direct peer-to-peer, zero-commission hyperlocal marketplace

Search verified professionals by service + pincode. Connect instantly. No middlemen, no commissions, no delays.

Target Users

Help Seekers (Customers)

  • Individuals needing on-demand household help
  • Plumbers, electricians, cleaners, tutors in minutes
  • Direct contact — no intermediary overhead
  • Transparent pricing before service begins
  • Hyperlocal — within their own neighbourhood

Service Professionals (Partners)

  • Skilled workers: plumbers, electricians, painters
  • Earn 100% of what they charge — no commissions
  • Flexible hours, build a local client base
  • Aadhaar + phone verification builds trust
  • Reach customers they'd never find otherwise

Key Achievements

1
API Design & Implementation

RESTful endpoints for provider registration and location-based discovery, with compound indexing for optimised queries.

2
Full-Stack Web Development

Responsive landing, service discovery, and partner onboarding pages using Next.js 16 and Tailwind CSS v4.

3
Cross-Platform Mobile App

React Native (Expo) app with bottom-tab navigation, search, and registration — targeting iOS and Android.

4
Database Integration

MongoDB Atlas with compound (pincode, service) index — significantly faster than full collection scans.

5
End-to-End Production Deployment

Live system: web on Vercel, API on Render, DB on MongoDB Atlas — single shared TypeScript backend.
